<h1 align="center">Hi 👋, I'm Gerardo Corral</h1>

<h3 align="center">AI & Data Science Engineer · Incoming PhD Researcher @ University of Bologna 🇮🇹</h3>

- 🎓 I am currently completing graduate studies at the **University of Bologna**, with a background spanning **Digital Transformation & Governance** and **Artificial Intelligence Engineering**.

- 🔬 I have been accepted into the **PhD Programme in Health, Safety and Green Systems at the University of Bologna**, starting in **November 2026**. My research direction focuses on the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence, environmental systems, digital twins, and climate resilience**.

- 🛰️ I currently work with **Hypermeteo** on applied AI, data science, and data engineering projects for meteorological and energy applications.

- 🧠 My recent work includes developing and validating **deep learning systems for solar irradiance nowcasting**, working with multi-source satellite, meteorological, and geospatial data, ConvLSTM architectures, distributed GPU training, HPC environments, full-domain inference, and scientific model validation.

- ⚙️ I also work on **data engineering and acquisition pipelines**, including APIs, high-frequency meteorological station data, automated validation, reproducible workflows, and production-oriented processing.

- 🌱 My main research interests include **Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Scientific Machine Learning, Spatiotemporal Forecasting, Digital Twins, Causal Inference, Causal Discovery, AI for environmental systems, and AI Governance**.

- 🔎 I am particularly interested in **causal inference and causal discovery** because prediction alone does not necessarily explain why an outcome occurs. Correlations are fundamental to data science, but treating them automatically as explanations can lead to weak or even harmful conclusions when technical systems are used to support decisions involving people, institutions, or complex physical environments.

- 🧩 My original academic background is in **Philosophy**, which still strongly influences how I approach AI: I am interested not only in whether a model works, but also in what it actually tells us, which assumptions make its conclusions possible, how reliable those conclusions are, and what happens when technical systems become part of real-world decision-making.

- 🛠️ Some of the technologies and methods I currently work with include **Python, PyTorch, Pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, DoWhy, SQL, R, Git, Linux, HPC, Distributed Computing, NetCDF, Deep Learning, Time Series, Geospatial Data, and Data Engineering**.
