
<h1 align="center">Hi 👋, I'm Gerardo Corral-Ruiz</h1>

<h3 align="center">
  PhD Researcher @ University of Bologna 🇮🇹 · Weather AI & Data Science
</h3>

<ul>
  <li>
    🎓 I am a <strong>PhD Researcher in Health, Safety and Green
    Systems at the University of Bologna</strong>, conducting research
    in collaboration with <strong>Hypermeteo</strong>. 
    I also earned my Master's degree in <strong>Governance 
    and Digital Innovation</strong> at the University of Bologna, 
    graduating with <strong>110/110 cum laude (highest honors)</strong>.
    I hold a bachelor's degree in <strong>Philosophy</strong> from
    <strong>Universidad Panamericana</strong> in Mexico, specializing
    in <strong>Contemporary Philosophy and Philosophy of Technology</strong>.
  </li>

  <li>
    🌍 My research interests focus on <strong>Weather AI, Machine Learning,
    Deep Learning, and climate resilience</strong>. I am particularly
    interested in understanding climate-related risks and developing
    intelligent tools for environmental monitoring, early warning,
    and adaptive planning.
  </li>

  <li>
    🚨 I aim to explore how AI can strengthen
    <strong>decision-support systems</strong> for public institutions,
    international organizations, and private-sector actors,
    supporting crisis management, strategic planning, and
    organizational decision-making. My goal is to help
    decision-makers anticipate environmental hazards,
    assess risks, and make better-informed decisions.
  </li>

  <li>
    🛰️ I am also interested in <strong>Earth observation,
    high-resolution environmental data, and spatiotemporal
    forecasting</strong>, exploring ways to improve the spatial
    detail and practical relevance of meteorological information.
  </li>

  <li>
    🧠 Previously, I worked at
    <a href="https://www.hypermeteo.com/en/"><strong>Hypermeteo</strong></a>,
    an Italian company specializing in high-resolution weather and
    climate intelligence, on applied AI, meteorological data
    science, and data engineering. My experience includes
    developing <strong>Deep Learning models for solar irradiance
    nowcasting</strong>, working with ConvLSTM, satellite and
    geospatial data, distributed GPU training, and
    <strong>High-Performance Computing (HPC)</strong>.
  </li>

  <li>
    ⚙️ My professional projects also include meteorological data
    acquisition and validation pipelines, API integration,
    and <strong>weather-related retail analytics</strong>,
    including an analytical project for a fashion company exploring relationships
    between weather conditions, sales, and discounts. I also developed an <strong>LLM-powered 
    Weather AI Agent</strong>, combining <strong>deterministic scientific tools
    with agentic AI workflows</strong>. Built with Python, FastAPI,
    LangGraph, and xarray/NetCDF, it supports meteorological data
    retrieval, statistical analysis, spatiotemporal comparisons,
    and GIS visualizations. Its agentic capabilities enable
    natural-language interaction and autonomous tool selection
    while keeping scientific computations deterministic.
  </li>

  <li>
    🌱 My broader scientific interests include
    <strong>Scientific Machine Learning, Digital Twins,
    Explainable AI, and AI Governance</strong>.
    I am also interested in exploring <strong>Causal AI
    and Causal Discovery</strong> in future research.
  </li>

  <li>
    🧩 <strong>Philosophy remains central to my academic identity.</strong>
    It shapes how I approach AI, scientific knowledge,
    the limits of prediction, and the relationship between
    technology, human judgment, and society.
  </li>

  <li>
    👨‍🏫 I am passionate about <strong>teaching philosophy and
    fostering critical thinking</strong>. I have taught at
    <a href="https://www.topuniversities.com/universities/universidad-panamericana">
    <strong>Universidad Panamericana</strong></a>,
    <a href="https://hybridge.education">
    <strong>Hybridge Education</strong></a>, and
    <a href="https://nsm.edu.mx/">
    <strong>Northridge School Mexico</strong></a>,
    across university and high-school education.
    I also enjoy developing courses and workshops,
    believing that future generations need both
    <strong>technological literacy and critical independence</strong>
    to question, understand, and transform their world.
  </li>
  
  <li>
    💻 During my Master's, I developed several interdisciplinary
    projects combining <strong>AI, Data Science, Cybersecurity,
    and Digital Innovation</strong>. Among these projects, my most
    significant work focused on
    <a href="https://github.com/GerardoACR/iiot_ids_project">
    <strong>Machine Learning for Industrial IoT Intrusion Detection</strong></a>,
    evaluating cybersecurity threats, class imbalance, and detection
    reliability. Other examples include an
    <a href="https://github.com/GerardoACR/cv-validator-and-analysis">
    <strong>LLM-assisted CV Processing System</strong></a>,
    integrating AI with deterministic validation workflows, and a
    <a href="https://github.com/GerardoACR/bologna-climate-health-risk">
    <strong>Climate–Health Risk Analysis in Bologna</strong></a>,
    examining temperature-related risks for vulnerable populations.
  </li>

  <li>
    🛠️ My technical toolkit includes
    <strong>Python, PyTorch, pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, LangChain,
    LangGraph, GeoPandas, GIS, SQL, R, Git, Linux, HPC, Distributed Computing,
    NetCDF, Geospatial Data, and Data Engineering</strong>.
  </li>
